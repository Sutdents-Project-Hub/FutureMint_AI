# FutureMint AI — 60 秒產品導覽

## Style Prompt

正式、清晰且帶有青春感的金融教育產品發表。以 FutureMint 的深靛黑介面作為主畫面，讓錄製的產品操作本身成為主角；鏡頭只在輸入、確認與關鍵數據處作精準推近，分析、學習與投資資料則完整呈現。視覺應讓人感到可信、好理解與有前進感，而非交易終端或浮誇科技展示。

## Visual System

- Background: `#14131F`; surface: `#1C1B2A`; raised surface: `#28263A`
- Primary accent: `#B8AEFF`; primary container: `#393368`; main text: `#FFF8EE`
- Typeface: `PingFang TC`, `Microsoft JhengHei`, system sans-serif
- Phase labels use a compact pill at the upper left; one concise title and a single lavender emphasis ring appear only when they clarify an action.
- Footage fills a 16:9 canvas. Use gentle 0.35–0.55s push/crossfade transitions and restrained camera pans; show dashboard, charts and learning pages full-frame when their information is the story.

## Avoid

- No music, sound effects, original recording audio, trading-terminal styling, neon gradients, or persistent glow.
- No continuous cursor chasing, jump cuts, fake presenters, or subtitle blocks (the narration will be supplied separately).
- No claims of investment returns or trading advice; the investment view remains explicitly educational and virtual.
